An early look at Audioforma

September 9, 2026

An early look at Audioforma — an audio-visualization and video-generation app I built from open-source libraries with the help of Claude AI. Feed it a track and it draws a reactive scene — spectrum bars, layered waveforms, a particle burst that breathes with the beat — then renders the whole thing out to a video file.

Audioforma, early look — blink-182, “After Midnight” driving the visuals.

It is still rough — the layout, colours and reactivity are all placeholders I am iterating on — but the pipeline works end to end: audio in, animated scene, video out. More on the render path and the presets in a later post.
