A first look at the playful Veo interface

September 7, 2026

Here's where playful Veo stands today. The whole screen is one drop target — a stage curtain, a ring of comedy-and-tragedy masks, and a single Open Video File button. Drop a clip and it starts playing straight away.

The point of the app is play: while a video runs you can throw a stack of real-time filters at it — mosaic, hex heat-map, chromatic glitch, halftone, shuffled-tile, edge trace — scrub around, and pull frames you like. Here's the full sweep, applied to Poet Anderson: The Dream Walker.

1 — The landing screen. The whole window is a drop zone.

2 — Pixelated edge effect, mid-playback at 5:08.

3 — Hexagon effect — the frame resampled onto a honeycomb.

4 — VHS glitch effect, VHS-style colour split and scanlines.

5 — Pixelated circle effect — the image rebuilt from a dot grid.

6 — Shuffled tiles effect — the frame sliced and nudged out of alignment.

7 — Edge trace effect — everything reduced to glowing outlines.

All seven run live at 100% speed, so you can flip between looks while the clip plays and grab whichever frame lands. More on the export path in the next post.
